# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 8, 2026_

**This app collects nothing.**

- It does not collect, store, or share any personal data.
- It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no
  tracking of any kind.
- It does not use the internet. It has no network permission and never
  connects to any server.
- The PDF files you open stay on your device. The app only reads the files
  you choose to open and only writes the files you choose to save.
- The list of recently opened files is stored on your device, inside the
  app. Nobody else can see it, and deleting the app deletes it.

If this ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change ships.

Questions: open an issue on the project's source repository.
